Our Policies - Digimarketing Web Development & SEO Services

We believe in transparency, trust, and professionalism. Here’s how we work with clients and protect your information.

1.  Privacy Policy

At Digimarketing, your privacy is important to us.

  • We collect personal information only for the purpose of providing our services.
  • Your details will never be sold, traded, or shared with third parties without your consent, except where required by law.
  • We use secure systems and encryption to protect your data.

2.  Terms of Service

By working with Digimarketing, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate information to help us deliver your web development or SEO project effectively.
  • Review and approve work in a timely manner to avoid delays.
  • Make payments as per the agreed schedule.

We agree to:

  • Deliver high-quality services in WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, and SEO as promised.
  • Maintain open and transparent communication throughout the project.
  • Meet deadlines wherever possible, unless unforeseen circumstances arise.

3.  Payment Policy

  • A deposit is required before work begins.
  • Remaining payment is due upon project completion, before the website goes live or final SEO reports are delivered.
  • Accepted payment methods include bank transfer, credit card, and PayPal.

4.  Refund Policy

  • Deposits are non-refundable once work has started.
  • If you cancel before work begins, we will issue a full refund.
  • In case of dissatisfaction, we will make reasonable revisions to meet the agreed scope before considering a partial refund

5.  Revision Policy

  • All projects include a set number of free revisions (as stated in the proposal).
  • Additional revisions may incur extra charges.

6.  Project Delivery

  • Websites are delivered fully functional and tested.
  • SEO campaigns include regular reports and updates.
  • Final files, credentials, and documentation are handed over once payment is completed.

7.  Contact Policy

We encourage clear and frequent communication. Our team is available via email, phone, or scheduled video calls during business hours (Singapore Time).
