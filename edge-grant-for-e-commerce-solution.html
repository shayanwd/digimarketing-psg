About the EDGE Grant

What is the EDGE Grant?

The EDGE Grant is Enterprise Singapore's single activity-based grant for Singapore companies starting new projects to upgrade capabilities and improve efficiency. From 30 September 2026 it replaces PSG, EDG and MRA for new applications.

If your online store project fits an eligible EDGE activity, you may receive up to 50% grant support. We will help you identify the right activity under Automation & Digitalisation and plan a WooCommerce, Shopify or custom store around it.

EDGE Grant Application Process

The EDGE Grant supports Singapore companies starting new projects across automation, digitalisation, innovation and more. Follow the steps below to plan your online store project.

Step # 1

Identify Your Business Priority

  • Determine the online store features, platform, integrations, and digital capabilities your business requires.
Step # 2

Select the EDGE Business Area and Activity

  • Use Enterprise Singapore's EDGE page or the BizSG recommender to find the activity that fits your project.
Step # 3

Get a Quotation

  • Obtain a quotation for your project from us.
Step # 4

Apply Under the EDGE Grant

  • From 30 September 2026, follow the application instructions for your chosen activity. Check the activity's conditions before paying a deposit or signing a contract.
Step # 5

Receive Approval and Implement

  • Tumhare existing description mein “Letter of Offer” wording hai. PDF specifically kehti hai is wording ko tabhi change karo agar EDGE activity page different terminology use karti hai.
Step # 6

Pay in Full and Submit Your Claim

  • EDGE is disbursed on a reimbursement basis. Claim after the activity is completed and full payment has been made.

Are you eligible?

EDGE Grant Eligibility

Eligibility depends on the EDGE activity you choose. EDGE is for Singapore companies starting new projects, and the support level differs for SMEs and non-SMEs.

Who Can Apply for the EDGE Grant?

Your business may be eligible if it meets the following criteria:
  • Registered and operating in Singapore.
  • Starting a new project that fits an EDGE activity.
  • Meets the conditions of the chosen activity.
  • Support for e-commerce solutions is up to 50%, subject to the activity conditions.
  • Total support stays within S$100,000 per year across all activities.
