PSG E-Commerce Grant Process

The Productivity Solutions Grant (PSG) supports Singapore companies in adopting digital solutions that enhance business efficiency. Eligible SMEs can receive up to 50% government funding when engaging PSG-approved vendors for services such as web design, eCommerce development, and digital marketing.

Step # 1

Identify Your E-Commerce Needs

  • Determine the online store features, platform, integrations, and digital capabilities your business requires.
Step # 2

Choose a Pre-Approved Solution and Vendor

  • Select an eligible e-commerce solution and approved vendor that matches your business requirements.
Step # 3

Get a Quotation

  • Obtain a quotation from the selected vendor for the applicable PSG-supported solution.
Step # 4

Submit Your PSG Application

  • Apply through the Business Grants Portal (BGP) using your Corppass account. Applications must be submitted before making payment or deposits to the vendor.
Step # 5

Receive Approval and Implement

  • Once approved, accept the Letter of Offer and proceed with the approved e-commerce solution according to the grant conditions.
Step # 6

Complete the Claim Process

  • After implementation, submit the required claim documents through BGP. Approved grant support is subsequently disbursed to the applicant.

Are you eligible?

PSG Grant Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for PSG grant support, your business must meet the applicable eligibility requirements set by Enterprise Singapore. The grant is designed to help eligible Singapore SMEs adopt digital solutions that improve productivity, efficiency, and business operations.

Who Can Apply for the PSG Grant?

Your business may be eligible if it meets the following criteria:
  • Registered and operating in Singapore.
  • Has at least 30% local shareholding.
  • Meets the applicable SME size requirements, based on group annual sales turnover or group employment.
  • Uses the approved digital solution in Singapore.
  • Selects an eligible PSG-supported solution and vendor.
  • Has not made any payment or deposit to the vendor or a third party before submitting the PSG application.

E-Commerce Eligibility

For e-commerce businesses, eligible digital solutions can help with online store development, e-commerce operations, and business digitalisation, subject to the specific solution's PSG eligibility.

Important Note

Important: PSG eligibility and funding support depend on the current grant requirements and the specific solution being adopted. Always confirm eligibility before committing to the project.

PSG is currently still being processed, but Enterprise Singapore has announced that PSG will cease on 29 September 2026. From 30 September 2026, businesses should apply for relevant business grant support under the EDGE Grant. Existing PSG submissions and ongoing PSG projects will continue to be processed and claims can still be submitted upon project completion.

From 30 September 2026, the EDGE Grant will provide business grant support across areas including automation and digitalisation. EDGE offers up to 70% support for SMEs and up to 50% for non-SMEs, depending on the eligible activity, with up to S$100,000 in total grant support per year across activities.
